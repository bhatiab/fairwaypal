# May: Best Golf Destinations in May
**Cheat sheet:** Top picks Pinehurst, Kiawah Island and the Algarve (all inside their published March to May best window), plus Scotland and Ireland (the May to September window opens, with long days). Myrtle Beach is still in its spring peak. Skip Scottsdale (the summer heat is arriving) and Florida (summer heat and storms begin).
**Angle:** "The best month nobody books." Shoulder pricing in Scotland and Ireland before the summer peak.

# June: Best Golf Destinations in June
**Cheat sheet:** Top picks Scotland and Ireland (longest daylight, up to 18 hours in Scotland), Bandon Dunes (dry season opens) and Pebble Beach (inside the May to October window; expect morning fog). Skip Scottsdale, Florida and Myrtle Beach (hot and humid).
**Angle:** "Go north or go links."

# July: Best Golf Destinations in July
**Cheat sheet:** Top picks Bandon Dunes (peak season), Scotland and Ireland (warmest month), Pebble Beach (cool and foggy, 55 to 65°F). Skip the Southeast and Southwest, and skip the Algarve (July and August are hot and crowded).
**Angle:** "Escape the heat." Bandon peak-season pricing note.

# August: Best Golf Destinations in August
**Cheat sheet:** Same top tier as July (Bandon, Scotland, Ireland, Pebble Beach). Value play: Myrtle Beach summer green fees for heat-tolerant groups. Skip the Algarve (peak heat and prices) and Scottsdale.
**Angle:** "Last call for links season." Book September trips now.
