# The Best Golf Destinations in December (2026 Honest Guide)

**Slug:** /blog/best-golf-destinations-december
**Meta title:** The Best Golf Destinations in December (2026 Honest Guide)
**Meta description:** An honest ranking of where to take a golf trip in December. Scottsdale, Florida, the Algarve and the Carolinas, plus the two holiday weeks to avoid and where to skip entirely.

December is the month where the weather and the calendar pull in opposite directions. The Sunbelt is properly pleasant, peak winter pricing hasn't fully landed, and the first two weeks are some of the quietest golf of the year. Then Christmas and New Year arrive, and prices, crowds and family logistics all spike at once. The short answer: go south or southwest, go early in the month, and you'll get a great trip at a fair price.

> **The cheat sheet**
> **Top three December picks:** Scottsdale (dry, mild, before the January peak), Florida (peak season starts, reliable warmth), the Algarve (mild and at its cheapest).
> **Strong second tier:** Kiawah Island, Myrtle Beach, Pinehurst (cool but very playable, winter rates).
> **Skip in December:** Scotland, Ireland (cold, very short days), Bandon Dunes (wet season), Pebble Beach (rainy season begins).
> **Go in the first two weeks.** Christmas through New Year is peak family travel pricing almost everywhere.

## 1. Scottsdale (dry, mild, before the January peak)

December is inside Scottsdale's October to April golf season. Days are mild and dry; mornings are cold enough that the first tee time of the day is a jacket round [VERIFY: typical December highs mid-60s°F, lows around 40°F]. The real advantage is timing: January and February are the busiest and most expensive months, so early December gets you peak-season conditions without peak-season rates. See our Scottsdale destination guide.

**Best for:** bachelor parties and milestone trips that want sunshine and nightlife in Old Town without paying January prices.

## 2. Florida (peak season starts)

Florida's golf season runs October to April, and December sits in the sweet spot: 60 to 75°F and low humidity. Hurricane season is over (it officially ends November 30). Streamsong, TPC Sawgrass and Innisbrook are all in good condition. Snowbird traffic ramps up after New Year, so early December is calmer. See our Florida destination guide.

**Best for:** East Coast and Midwest groups who want an easy flight and guaranteed warmth.

## 3. The Algarve (mild and at its cheapest)

December is low season in the Algarve. It's cooler than spring or autumn and wetter than October [VERIFY: typical December highs around 16°C; expect some wet days], but courses stay open and villa prices are at their annual low. Monte Rei and Quinta do Lago are bookable at off-peak rates. See our Algarve destination guide.

**Best for:** European groups and value-conscious partner trips. Partners get quiet old towns and long seafood lunches rather than beach days.

## 4. Kiawah Island (cool, quiet, winter rates)

Kiawah in December is mild by northern standards, but the Ocean Course can be properly windy. Green fees and The Sanctuary drop to winter rates, and Charleston dresses up for the holidays, which partners tend to love. See our Kiawah Island destination guide.

**Best for:** couples and mixed groups who care more about Charleston than about shorts weather.

## 5. Myrtle Beach (best value in the country)

Winter rounds are very playable at Myrtle Beach (expect 45 to 55°F on cooler days) and pricing is about as low as it gets for quality courses. The beach is for walking, not swimming. See our Myrtle Beach destination guide.

**Best for:** budget-led groups who'd rather play 4 rounds in cool weather than 2 in the sun.

## 6. Pinehurst (crisp, quiet, discounted)

Winter golf at Pinehurst is possible, with temperatures around 45 to 60°F and green fees well down from spring and fall. The village is quiet and festive. Layer up and accept that one round might be cold. See our Pinehurst destination guide.

**Best for:** heritage-loving groups who want No. 2 at a lower price and don't mind a fleece.

## Destinations to skip in December

- **Scotland and Ireland:** cold, wet, and daylight is very short [VERIFY: around 7 hours in Scotland in mid-December]. Many courses run limited hours. Push to May to September.
- **Bandon Dunes:** Pacific Northwest wet season. The resort is open year-round, but expect wind and rain.
- **Pebble Beach:** December to February is the rainy season on the Monterey Peninsula. October is the better bet.

## The two holiday weeks

Christmas week and New Year's week are peak family travel in Scottsdale, Florida, Myrtle Beach and Kiawah. Flights, hotels and restaurants all get harder and more expensive. If your group has any flexibility, the first two weeks of December are the target: winter-season conditions, pre-holiday prices.

## Booking timing

Book 30 to 60 days out for early December. For anything touching Christmas or New Year, book 90 days out or more [VERIFY against current hotel availability].

## FAQ

**What is the best place to play golf in December?**
Scottsdale and Florida are the most reliable December picks: dry or warm, inside their peak golf seasons, and cheaper in early December than in January. The Algarve is the best-value European option. Avoid Scotland, Ireland, Bandon Dunes and Pebble Beach in December.

**Is December a good month for a golf trip?**
Yes, if you go south and go early. The first two weeks of December are quiet and fairly priced in the Sunbelt. Christmas and New Year weeks are peak family travel and the most expensive weeks of the winter.

**Is Scottsdale or Florida better in December?**
Scottsdale is drier, with cooler mornings. Florida is warmer and more humid. Scottsdale wins on nightlife and desert courses; Florida wins on easy East Coast flights and warmth.

**Can you play golf in the Carolinas in December?**
Yes. Kiawah, Myrtle Beach and Pinehurst are all playable in December, with cool temperatures and winter green fee rates. Expect jacket weather and the occasional cold, windy day.

**Should I avoid Christmas week for a golf trip?**
For a group trip, yes. Christmas and New Year weeks are peak family travel pricing at most warm-weather destinations, and partners usually have family commitments anyway.
